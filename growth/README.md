# Growth + Outreach

Campus launch plans, social content, referral system, and metrics.

## What belongs here

- `launch-plan/` — City-by-city rollout: target colleges, ambassador recruitment, timeline
- `ambassadors/` — Student ambassador program: application, onboarding, incentives, tracking
- `partnerships/` — University intl. offices, student associations, orientation programs
- `social-content/` — Instagram/LinkedIn/Twitter content calendar, testimonial videos, graphics
- `referral/` — "Invite a friend" mechanics, rewards (profile boost, early access), tracking
- `press/` — Press kit, launch announcement, media outreach list
- `metrics/` — North star: meaningful connections created; leading indicators: signups, profile completion, first message, first meetup, 7-day retention

## Rollout Stages

| Stage | Target | Channels | Goal |
|-------|--------|----------|------|
| 1. Beta | 3-5 colleges in 1 city | Ambassadors, WhatsApp groups | 500 users, density > 20/college |
| 2. City | All colleges in city | Partnerships, orientation, social | 3,000 users, active communities |
| 3. India | Top 20 student cities | National ambassadors, press | 25,000 users |
| 4. Global | Top destination countries | Local teams, partnerships | Country-by-country |

## Ambassador Incentives

- Early access to features
- "Verified Ambassador" badge
- Swag (stickers, tote, hoodie at milestones)
- Leadership certificate
- Direct line to product team
